About the Movie
The school for good and evil The School for Good and Evil is a dark fairy-tale fantasy novel written by Soman Chainani. It centers on two radically different best friends who are whisked away to a legendary academy where ordinary children are trained to become fairy-tale heroes or villains.

Why I Like This Movie
I like The School for Good and Evil because it flips traditional fairy-tale rules upside down by showing that true goodness and wickedness come from a person's choices and actions rather than their outward appearance. The deep bond of friendship between Sophie and Agatha—who are unexpectedly swapped into opposite schools—proves that loyalty and sacrifice matter much more than fitting into a neat label. Coupled with its dazzling magical world, stunning visual effects, and a fun cast like Charlize Theron and Kerry Washington, the movie delivers an entertaining and heartfelt reminder that nobody is strictly cut-and-dried as just good or just evil.

Conclusion
The story of The School for Good and Evil concludes with the power of friendship prevailing over traditional fairy-tale romance. In the first installment, after defeating the dark School Master, Sophie is fatally wounded, but Agatha saves her life with a kiss on the cheek—proving that true platonic love can break magical curses. The two best friends return home to Gavaldon, though a message from Prince Tedros hints that their adventures are far from over. By the end of the full six-book saga, Tedros reclaims the throne of Camelot with Agatha as his queen, while Sophie embraces her destiny as the new Dean of the School for Evil, establishing a new era where Good and Evil coexist through empathy and self-choice.
